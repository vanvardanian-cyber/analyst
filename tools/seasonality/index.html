How to get the files:
  1. Gate 1: Helium 10 → Cerebro → Analyze Keywords → marketplace Amazon.de → parent keyword (+ 2–3 children, comma-separated — one search credit) → chart icon next to Search Volume → period “All time” → download.
  2. Gate 2: search the parent keyword on Amazon.de → run Xray on the results page → Export → CSV. Export the full page; the tool deduplicates and filters.
  3. Gate 3: FBA fee from Xray or the Revenue Calculator; EXW from supplier quotes; freight from your forwarder’s rate × unit volume.
Method & thresholds: classical multiplicative decomposition, seasonal strength and year consistency after Hyndman & Athanasopoulos (otexts.com/fpp3); selection gates follow the EaseStore workbook (page revenue, review moat, Amazon presence, CN/HK share, fresh entrants); price corridor and margin floor after common practitioner benchmarks (Freedom Ticket) — tightened for German VAT reality.
